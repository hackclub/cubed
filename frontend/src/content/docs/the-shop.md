## Moles

Moles are Cubed's official currency. You get 5 moles for every approved hour. Once approved,
you can spend these moles in the shop.

Fun fact, in chemistry the "mole" is a unit of measurement, the base unit in the International
System of Units for amount of substance. You might see things like atoms, molecules, ions and
electrons being calculated in moles.

## Prizes

The shop will have science prizes like Newton's cradles, Rubik's cubes and more. All prizes are
completely free!

You can suggest prizes inside of the “cubed shop suggestion” canvas on Slack. We can’t
guarantee every prize but we’ll try our best.

## Buying something

1. Open the Shop from your dashboard.
2. Pick a prize.
3. Enter your shipping info.
4. Check your order summary.
5. Hit confirm. Moles are only taken when you confirm.

## Shipping

- Depending on the item, it may take some time to fulfill it! We are based in the USA, so
  shipping and ordering prizes internationally can be hard! We’ll try our best to get your
  prize within 5 weeks after being bought from the shop. But we’ll try our best to get it done
  ASAP.
- Your shipping info is only used to send your prize and is never stored on the Cubed
  website!

Have a problem with an order? Ask in #cubed.
