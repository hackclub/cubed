## Welcome to Cubed!

[Cubed](https://cubed.hackclub.com) is a Hack Club YSWS (You Ship We Ship)
program where teens ages 13-18 build cool science related coding projects
and get supa cool science prizes for free!!

From Oct 9th to November 9th, you’ll spend the next few weeks building
projects, earning moles, making friends, and hopefully getting the
sciencey prizes of your dreams. I’ve always wanted a space for Hack Club
members and programmers to nerd out about science. And I hope this YSWS
provides that to you. This page is a short guide on how to participate in
Cubed!

**How it works:**

1. Ideate
2. Build your project
3. Submit your project
4. Share with the world (aka online)
5. Get your prizes

## Step 1: The idea

Have you ever watched some cool shows like Mark Rober or MythBusters as a
child and wondered, “How the helly did they build that?” Well, now I want
you to be the one to make super wild science projects. But this time with
code.

### What makes a good Cubed project

Now, your project idea can be as complex or simple as you want. In fact,
if you're new to coding or trying out different tools, it should be really
simple! A lot of cool science ideas involve learning new programs,
technologies and languages that can be very different from what the
average web developer uses. So don’t be afraid to start small.

Your only goal is to build something science related, ideally a project
that helps others understand, recreate, or learn new things about science!
Research projects also count, but keep in mind that it should be research
about science.

### What counts as science?

Science is a pretty vast field, and I can't possibly list each one. But,
some common topics are Space & astronomy, Physics, Chemistry, Biology,
Earth, geology, Math, Health / medical, and Math & theory focused
computer science.

If you have more questions, ask in Slack or check out the [What counts as
science](./what-counts) docs for a more detailed answer.

## Step 2: Build your project

Before you build your project, make sure to check on Slack if it counts as
a science project. Also feel free to talk about your idea or your plans
for building it in the **#cubed** channel on Slack. We might be able to
give you useful tools or tips on how to make your idea come to life.

Make sure to follow all the steps below so your project will be ready to
submit!

### 1. Create a GitHub repository

Before you can even start your project, you’ll need a GitHub account.
GitHub is a platform where you can store your code, track changes over
time, and share code with others. Please commit every single hour! If you
don't commit regularly we may have to deflate your hours. We use GitHub to
keep track of how much work you did over time.

You can find the tutorial covering the basics of GitHub [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9u2yZvsqHA&t=78s).

### 2. Set up Hackatime

We use Hackatime to track the time you spend coding. Not sure how to set
up Hackatime, or having issues with it? Check out the [Hackatime
documentation](https://hackatime.hackclub.com/docs) or this [video tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grriwsX5mIo).

### 3. Follow the rules

There are a couple rules about AI, tutorial usage and cheating. In short,
you shouldn't overuse AI for your projects, you shouldn't completely copy
a tutorial, and you cannot fake your Hackatime hours.

You can learn more about the rules [here](./rules).

## Step 3: Ship your project & submit

Shipping is the most important part of your project, almost as important
as the entire project itself. So please make sure to read the [shipping
guidelines](./shipping) in depth.

After you ship your project, you can submit it for review. Once a reviewer
approves it, you earn moles (our currency) for the hours you put in. If
you don’t ship your project properly, it will be rejected.

Here's a short checklist on how to ship:

1. Open source your project in a public repository
2. Have a README that has enough info
3. Have a live demo

## Step 4: Share your project with the world !!

Alright, to be completely honest, this step is optional, but highly
recommended. Once you're done with your project, please share it with the
world, both online and in person! Show your friends, family, or even
teachers.

Oftentimes, especially if you're a beginner, you compare yourself to super
complex projects and doubt your own skills. It's really easy to forget
that so many people find coding cool and impressive. You having the
initiative, skills and work ethic to spend your free time coding personal
projects is really freaking cool :3

Don’t forget to share it online! Share your finished projects in
**#cubed** on Slack. I want to see what you built! We also have a channel
called **#ship** specifically for sharing shipped projects. Hacker News,
Reddit, X/Twitter and other online communities are also great places to
post.

## Step 5: Get your prizes

After you submit your project and it’s approved by reviewers, you get
moles! Moles are Cubed’s official currency. You get 5 moles per approved
hour, and you can spend these moles in the shop.

The shop will have many different prizes like Newton's cradles, Rubik's
cubes and more. All these prizes are completely free! Check out the
[Shipping docs](./shipping) for more details.

Have any more questions? Check out the [FAQ](./faq).
