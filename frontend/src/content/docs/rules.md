### Don’t do fraud!

Please don’t try to cheat or fake the hours you spend on your project.
Hack Club doesn’t have infinite money. We're a nonprofit, and all our
prizes are either bought by us or donated to us. All our programs are
completely free and we want to fairly award people who ship projects!

Keep in mind we have advanced tooling to catch it if you cheat your hours.
Fraud results in a permanent ban from the program, and potentially Hack
Club as a whole. Don't risk it.

### AI & plagiarism

Please don’t plagiarize or vibe code your project. These can get your
project permanently rejected. We want people to ship their own original
projects.

You cannot:

- Submit a project built by someone else as your own
- Use AI to generate your entire project without any review, polish or
  iteration
- Follow a tutorial almost exactly 1:1 and submit it as your own

It's completely fine, and even recommended, to follow a tutorial! But you
must add a couple of features that you built completely by yourself, or at
the very least, change how some of the code is written. Your final project
should not be a 1:1 copy of a tutorial. You can build on it and add extra
features, swap your dataset or API, or change the library.

Your code should look like yours, not like the tutorial's.
