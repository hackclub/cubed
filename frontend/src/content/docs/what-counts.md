Here are some common science topics your project can focus on:

- **Space & astronomy:** visualize planets, stars, orbits or rockets, or
  analyze public data about stars.
- **Physics:** make your own physics engine, or simulate gravity, motion,
  light or sound.
- **Chemistry:** tools that help solve chemistry equations, or a game
  where you use chemistry knowledge to solve problems.
- **Biology:** analyze or simulate cells, genetics, animals, the human
  body or evolution.
- **Earth science & geology:** weather, earthquakes, volcanoes, climate,
  oceans, rocks.
- **Math:** calculators, graphing tools, geometry visualizers.
- **Health & medicine:** an interactive diagram of how bodies work, or an
  AI that classifies diseases.
- **Math & theory-focused computer science:** machine learning, quantum
  computing, and very complex algorithms (like cryptography).

Keep in mind that these topics aren't the only options. If your idea
doesn't fit, or you're not sure it counts, ask in **#cubed** and we'll
tell you!

## About computer science projects

Every coding project is technically computer science, so "it's code" isn't
enough. For your CS project to count, the math or theory has to be the
point of the project, or at least a large part of it.

- Machine learning & deep learning: counts as long as you're building or
  training models. Just using AI API credits doesn't count.
- Algorithms: intermediate to advanced algorithms count, but please check
  in **#cubed** on Slack first.
