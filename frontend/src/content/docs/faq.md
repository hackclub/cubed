### Who can join?

Teens 13–18. It's completely free!

### When does Cubed run?

We run from October 9 to November 9, 2026. Technically, any hours
you've logged on Hackatime since June 1, 2026 count.

### Does my project count as science?

Check the [What counts as science](./what-counts) page. Still not sure?
Ask in **#cubed** before you start building.

### Can I submit more than one project?

Yes! As many as you want.

### Can I double dip?

Nope. Once you've submitted your project, you can't submit those same
hours to Cubed again.

### How long do reviews take?

We aim to keep reviews under 2 weeks. We'll ping you in **#cubed-ship**
when your project is reviewed!

### How many moles do I get?

5 moles per approved hour.

### Can I use AI?

Keep it under 30% of your project. AI slop or heavy AI usage will be
rejected.

### Can I follow a tutorial?

Yes! Just add your features.

### Where do I get help?

Ask in **#cubed** on Slack!
